## About ReceiptFlow

ReceiptFlow is a finance application designed to help small businesses, accountants, and individuals manage their receipts and track their spending.

The aim is to make it easier to organise receipts, calculate VAT, and understand monthly expenses in one place.

## Features

- Receipt Uploads
  - Upload receipts in formats such as PDF, PNG, and JPG.

- Receipt Data Extraction
  - Extract information from receipts to reduce manual data entry.

- Automatic VAT Calculation
  - Calculate VAT using information taken from receipts.

- Receipt Verification
  - If a receipt is unclear or difficult to read, users can check and confirm the extracted information.

- Monthly Spending
  - View expenses across different spending categories.

- Expense Management
  - Keep receipts and expense records organised.

- Bank Statement Support
  - Bank statement features are planned for future development.

## Who Is It For?

- Small businesses that want to manage receipts and track expenses.
- Accountants who need to review receipts and VAT information.
- Individuals who want to track personal spending.
